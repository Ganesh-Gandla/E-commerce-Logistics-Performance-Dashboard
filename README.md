# 📦 E-commerce Logistics Performance Analytics Dashboard

## 📖 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze the operational performance of an e-commerce logistics business. The dashboard provides valuable insights into shipment performance, delivery efficiency, customer orders, revenue trends, and regional operations, enabling stakeholders to make informed, data-driven decisions.

The project demonstrates the complete data analysis workflow—from data cleaning and transformation to visualization and business insight generation—using Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

E-commerce companies handle thousands of shipments every day across multiple regions and shipping methods. Monitoring logistics performance is essential for improving operational efficiency, reducing delivery delays, and enhancing customer satisfaction.

The objective of this dashboard is to:

* Monitor key logistics and operational KPIs
* Analyze shipment and delivery trends
* Identify operational bottlenecks
* Compare regional performance
* Support data-driven business decisions

---

## ❓ Business Questions

This dashboard is designed to answer the following business questions:

* How many shipments have been processed?
* What is the overall delivery performance?
* Which regions generate the highest shipment volume?
* Which regions experience the most delivery delays?
* How do shipment trends change over time?
* Which shipping methods perform the best?
* What are the key operational KPIs that management should monitor?

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive visualizations such as:

* KPI Cards
* Bar Charts
* Line Charts
* Decomposition Tree
* Interactive Slicers
* Dynamic Filtering

Users can explore the data interactively to analyze logistics performance across different business dimensions.

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks important business metrics, including:

* Total Shipments
* Total Orders
* Revenue
* Delivery Performance
* Shipment Trends
* Regional Performance

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

The dashboard helps stakeholders identify:

* High-performing and low-performing operational regions.
* Shipment trends across different time periods.
* Distribution of orders across multiple business dimensions.
* Operational performance patterns that may require attention.
* Areas where logistics efficiency can be improved.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis, organizations can consider:

* Optimizing logistics operations in underperforming regions.
* Monitoring delivery trends to proactively reduce delays.
* Improving resource allocation based on shipment volume.
* Regularly tracking operational KPIs to improve efficiency.
* Using interactive dashboards for continuous performance monitoring.
